I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Graduate Center, CUNY, working under the supervision of Professor Theodore Joyce. My research is in applied microeconomics, with a focus on higher education policy and student outcomes.

I am currently writing a paper on CUNY's SEEK program, a need-based bundled-aid intervention that admits students below their campus's own academic bar, asking whether its effect on student outcomes held up after COVID changed both the population it serves and the admissions rule that selects them. More broadly, I work with large-scale administrative data to evaluate education policy, using tools from applied microeconometrics — selection models, decomposition methods, and sensitivity analysis for unobserved confounding.
